Services Standard Features

We operate nationwide as we optimise to fulfill our customers request. Our vessels currently sail eastbound, such as Maluku and Papua Province, which covers Obi Island, Morowali, Wayame, Weda Bay, Halmahera, and many other islands in the area.

Safety First

We make sure installation of pipes for the passing of the fuel is appropriate and we make sure all the requirements are met and the equipments and set ups are verified.

Constant Maintenance

We are not just bounded by docking inspection checklist-to-do when our fleets are docked, instead we put out our own team of service maintenance on board to perform Weekly check.

Zero Spill

We are striving for reduction of all kinds of pollutants by undertaking identification of all such activities of the ship and its pipelines that are detrimental to the marine environment.

Zero Accident

We continuously perform safety management skills of personnel On board, including safety drills related both to safety and environmental protection.
