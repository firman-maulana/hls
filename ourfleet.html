Our Fleet

We own, manage and operates our vessels with Wholeheartedly and capable human resources.

Harpa Lintas Samudera owns, manage and operates two small takers and ready to serve all over indonesian waters. Our vessels are also equiped with legal certificates and qualifies for safety approval

MT. GRIYA FLORES

MT. GRIYA FLORES (IMO: 8888757) is a Oil/Chemical Tanker that was built in 1994, and assembled with double hull and bottom. She is sailing under the flag of Indonesia, and have sucessfully Qualified for PSA (Pertamina Safety Approval).

Her gross tonage is 1372 tonnes and dead weight tonage is 2076 tonnes DWT with carrying capacity of 2091 CBM and her current draught is reported to be 4.9 meters. Her length overall (LOA) is 78.50 meters, her width is 12 meters and depth is 5.6 meters

MT. CAHAYA MULYA 88

MT. CAHAYA MULYA 88 (IMO: 9058892), built in 1993, is a Oil Products Tanker that was assembled with single hull and double bottom. She is sailing under the flag of Indonesia, and have sucessfully Qualified for PSA (Pertamina Safety Approval).

Her gross tonage is 1006 tonnes and dead weight tonage is 1853 tonnes DWT with carrying capacity of 2178 CBM and her current draught is reported to be 4.6 meters. Her length overall (LOA) is 75.02 meters, her width is 11.2 meters and depth is 5.1 meters.
